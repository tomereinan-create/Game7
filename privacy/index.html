The short version

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no ads, no ad trackers, no tracking pixels.
  • Your progress stays in your own browser. We never receive it.
  • We count how the game is used with anonymous, cookieless analytics. Nothing is stored on your device for that, and you can switch it off below.

Your saves

Your campaigns, club name, stars and settings are kept in this browser's local storage on your device. They are never sent to us. Clearing your browser data deletes them. A save code is your progress written out as text so you can move it to another browser yourself; anyone you give a save code to can load that progress.

Analytics

%HTML_ANALYTICS%

Your save data is never read for analytics. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, or you switch analytics off below, none of the above is loaded or sent.

Analytics in this browser: on

Turning them off keeps one small "off" setting in this browser so the choice is remembered.

Hosting and fonts

The game is served by Cloudflare, which handles your connection (and so your IP address) to deliver the pages, as any web host does. Everything the game needs — its code, its pictures and its fonts — comes from this site itself; apart from the Cloudflare counter above, no other company is contacted to draw the page.

Children

The game is made for a general audience. It asks for no name, email or other personal information, and it shows no ads.

Your rights and contact

Because we hold no account or personal profile, there is usually nothing we can look up about you. If you have a question or a request about your data, write to %SITE_CONTACT%.
